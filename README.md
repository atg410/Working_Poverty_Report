# Working Poverty Report

Quantifying the Working Poor in the Netherlands
This project was developed for the course Programming for Economists (VU, 2025) and focuses on analyzing the prevalence and patterns of the working poor in the Netherlands using public data from CBS (Statistics Netherlands). Our primary aim was to examine how the proportion of working poor individuals—those who are employed but live below the poverty line—varies across city sizes.

🔍 Research Motivation
While unemployment is often the main focus in times of crisis, the working poor receive significantly less policy attention. Yet this group faces real financial instability, mental health risks, and long-term socioeconomic vulnerability. In this study, we investigate how structural inequality persists even within the employed population.

🌍 Findings
Our results show that larger cities tend to have higher proportions of working poor, and that the pandemic has had positive effects on this group. These insights can support more targeted policymaking and a broader understanding of economic vulnerability in Dutch society.
